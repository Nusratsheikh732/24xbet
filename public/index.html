Game Rules (3,600 Ways)

  • Wild Bounty Showdown is a 6-reel (3 rows in reels 1 and 6, 4 rows in reels 2 and 5, 5 rows in reels 3 and 4) video slot featuring Gold Framed Symbols and increasing multiplier.
  • Gold Framed symbols appear on reels 3 and 4 only.
  • During any spin, Gold Framed symbols involved in a win transform into WILD symbols for the next cascade!
  • Win Multiplier starts at x1 (or x8 in Free Spins) and doubles after every cascading win up to x1024!
  • Base Bet is 20 for 3,600 ways. Way wins are multiplied by (Bet Amount / 20) and the active Multiplier.
